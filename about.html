OUR STORY

Rooted in Kashmir.
Built for more.

Steel Syndicate started in Sanat Nagar Industrial Estate, Srinagar, with a direct belief: steelwork should be dependable, well-made and built to last. Over nearly three decades, that belief has shaped every truss, structure, girder and custom fabrication we have undertaken.

Today, clients across industrial, commercial, residential and government sectors rely on our team to deliver high-quality steel solutions with clear communication, safety and an unwavering focus on the finish.
